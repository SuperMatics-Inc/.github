# SuperMatics

SuperMatics develops the software and intelligence layer to build the future of superconductivity. We are based in Berkeley, California.

## Public research

- [eliashberg-tc-bounds](https://github.com/SuperMatics-Inc/eliashberg-tc-bounds): Lean proofs and certificates for bounds on the Eliashberg critical temperature. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.20496) · [Software](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24270482)
- [eliashberg-uniqueness](https://github.com/SuperMatics-Inc/eliashberg-uniqueness): Supplement to the article on thermodynamic uniqueness in multiband and anisotropic Eliashberg models without Coulomb repulsion. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24326099) · [Software](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24326101)
- [mustar-finite-window](https://github.com/SuperMatics-Inc/mustar-finite-window): Linear and global stability in a Coulomb-penalized Matsubara spin chain. [Report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24326111) · [Software](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24326113)
- [gl-vortex-morse-indices](https://github.com/SuperMatics-Inc/gl-vortex-morse-indices): Certificates for the Morse indices of nonmagnetic Ginzburg–Landau vortices of degrees four through eight. [Report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24326103) · [Software](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24326105)
- [lll-lattice-stability](https://github.com/SuperMatics-Inc/lll-lattice-stability): Certificates for non-hexagonal spectral stability in the lowest-Landau-level equation. [Report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24326107) · [Software](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24326109)

Other company code is private.
